10 reasons parents choose Cambridge

A world-class education trusted in 160+ countries, now for your child at Sloka International School, Manneguda.

  1. 01

    Recognised by universities everywhere

    Accepted in India and by 2,670+ universities worldwide, including 1,070+ in the USA.

  2. 02

    One clear path from age 3 to 19

    Early Years, Primary, Lower Secondary, IGCSE and A Level. Each stage builds on the last.

  3. 03

    Understanding, not memorising

    Active learning and real-world examples build deep understanding of every subject.

  4. 04

    Skills for life, not just exams

    Critical thinking, communication, problem-solving and confidence for a changing world.

  5. 05

    Real choice of subjects

    70+ IGCSE and 55+ A Level subjects, so your child can follow their strengths.

  6. 06

    Fair, meaningful assessment

    Exams test thinking and understanding, in clear language that is fair to every learner.

  7. 07

    Every child supported

    An inclusive approach that respects different abilities and helps each child grow their own way.

  8. 08

    Teachers backed by Cambridge

    Teachers get Cambridge training and evidence-based resources, which means better classrooms.

  9. 09

    A global learning community

    10,000+ schools in 160+ countries, with nearly 2 million learners every year.

  10. 10

    160+ years of trust

    Part of the University of Cambridge, setting international standards since 1864.
